Getting Started

Do I need an account?

No account is required for most Carzzy Apps. Open a game or tool from the homepage and start using it right away.

How do I use an app?

Choose an app from Games & Apps, then follow the controls shown inside that app. Each project has its own rules and settings.

Are the apps mobile-friendly?

Many Carzzy Apps support smartphones. If a game feels cramped, try landscape orientation or a larger browser window.

Where can I find new projects?

New releases and updates appear on the Carzzy Apps homepage. Look for the NEW label in the games and apps list.

Games & Apps

A game or tool is not responding.

Refresh the page and make sure your browser is up to date. Closing other tabs can also help apps that use audio, timers, or animation.

Can I save or share my work?

Saving and sharing depends on the individual app. Check for a Save, Share, or Copy Link control within the project you are using.

How do I report a bug?

Include the app name, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, your browser, and a screenshot when you contact support.

How can I suggest a feature?

Use the contact page to describe the idea and which game or app it would improve. Suggestions for future Jeopardy questions are welcome too.

Leaderboards

How do I submit a score?

Finish the relevant challenge, then use the submission control provided by that game. Follow the on-screen instructions carefully.

My score is missing.

Confirm that you completed the submission step and entered your name correctly. If it still does not appear, contact us with the game and approximate submission time.

Can I change a leaderboard entry?

Leaderboard entries may be reviewed for mistakes or inappropriate content. Contact support with the entry details so it can be checked.

Why is my result different?

Some games use timing, device input, or browser behavior when calculating results. Use the same settings and check the game instructions before retrying.

Troubleshooting

The page will not load.

Check your connection, reload the page, and try a private browser window. A temporary browser extension or cached file may be interfering.

Audio or controls are not working.

Check your device volume and browser permissions, then interact with the page once before starting audio. Reloading usually restores a stuck control.

The layout looks incorrect.

Zoom your browser back to 100% and try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. On a phone, rotate to landscape for games designed around a wide screen.

What details should I send with a report?

Tell us the page or app, device, browser version, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message you saw.
